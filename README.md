# Minimal Research Theme :microscope:

Just a plain, simple and elegant one-page theme for research/academia.

## Exclude your own visits from analytics

After deploying, open https://shreesha00.github.io/?analytics=off once in each
browser you use on your laptop and phone. A confirmation appears when the
preference is saved, and the setting is removed from the URL so you can share
the normal website link. Google Analytics will not load in that browser on
subsequent visits, including after switching Wi-Fi or cellular networks.

This preference is stored in local storage for the site's origin. Repeat the
opt-out for each browser/profile or different site domain, and after clearing
site data. Private browsing does not preserve it after the private session ends;
browsers may also clear stored data automatically. If storage is blocked, the
confirmation explains that the opt-out only applies to the current visit.

To enable analytics again, visit https://shreesha00.github.io/?analytics=on.
This affects future visits only; existing analytics data is unchanged.

## Just turn the knobs!
* For those who just want a quick website, just make changes to style.js.
